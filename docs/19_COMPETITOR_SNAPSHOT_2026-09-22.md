# Competitor Snapshot — September 22, 2026

This is a dated snapshot of the six visible open pull requests in the official jam repository. New entries may appear before close. Recheck the official pull-request list before finalizing the originality statement.

## Visible field

### The Last Light

Haunted toy-theatre tower defense. A moving lantern reveals otherwise invulnerable ghosts and strengthens defenses, but also accelerates enemies in the beam. Nested toy enemies and build-site strategy are part of its stated hook.

### Hungry for Trouble

Grocery-store shooter/maze. A vacuum consumes crumbs that mark escape routes and replenishes ammunition; a surveillance visor shifts play into first-person combat. The submission also describes campaign, co-op, versus, and leaderboard content.

### SYZYGY ORBIT SNAP

One-thumb orbital timing game. The craft auto-orbits and the player presses SNAP at a sun-object-craft alignment, with a lock/freeze and micro-bonus layer.

### BOLTWORKS

Isometric tank combat. The tank is assembled from 404-generated modules and visibly disassembles/rebuilds between waves as hull, barrel, and armor components change.

### Guardians of the Rock

Historical flying-boat simulation. Players use period radar, identify contacts, report by radio, hunt submarines, choose crew positions, and set depth charges.

### LIGHTKEEPER

Lighthouse resource/time-management game. The player sails to refill lamp oil while the rotating beam guides ships; running dry loses ships in the dark.

## Gaps SECOND HANDS targets

- No visible entry centers on recording human play and turning it into cooperative embodied agents.
- No visible entry uses simultaneous past selves as the entire puzzle language.
- No visible entry presents a demonstration-programming mechanic where the player creates each role by performing it.
- The compact, non-combat temporal puzzle genre is underrepresented in this snapshot.

## Avoid accidental overlap

- Do not pitch modular disassembly/rebuild as the main innovation; BOLTWORKS already owns that visible claim.
- Do not make the game primarily a timing-only one-button experience; SYZYGY occupies that lane.
- Do not rely on light/dark resource tension as the primary rule; The Last Light and LIGHTKEEPER use light centrally.
- Do not compete on campaign length or feature count; Hungry for Trouble already emphasizes breadth.

## Strategic implication

SECOND HANDS should win attention through:

1. an instantly readable first echo,
2. a visually synchronized four-body final solution,
3. an art-directed clockwork diorama,
4. a concise originality statement about programming agents by demonstration,
5. unusually strong testing and production receipts.
