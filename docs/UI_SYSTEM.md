# Typography and interface

Display: locally bundled Cormorant Garamond. Interface: locally bundled Manrope. Both are SIL Open Font License; license files ship beside fonts. Title uses deliberately spaced capitals, a cyan double slash, ivory lettering and substantial empty space. Do not rasterize text into media.

Desktop: title copy begins at 7.3% width, menu is a quiet 300px column; wide scene remains visible at right. One cyan leading edge indicates the primary action. Avoid excessive glowing boxes and icon clutter. Footer and edition metadata are subordinate.

Phone 390×844: title moves to the lower half with a darker readability gradient; character is framed above and behind it. Main actions are at least 58px tall. Gameplay uses 64px pause, 72px ACT and a 98px joystick; control cards are 92px. Settings have labels and values, visible keyboard focus, native range semantics and switch labels. Pause/how/settings/results keep their action inside the viewport.

HUD: sector/objective upper left, desktop sector route top, counters lower left, three control cards bottom center. Only BRIGHTNESS is available. Socket feedback is attached to the projected world position. Real keyboard alternative avoids requiring dragging for access.

Focus is constrained to open menus and restored on closing a panel. Escape pauses/resumes or returns. Reduced motion removes video and camera interpolation. Mute and bus levels persist. No runtime implementation or debugging terminology is shown in the gameplay flow; the creation log is the explicit provenance exception.
