# CTRL//REALITY art direction

“A cinematic impossible archive built from obsidian glass, ivory ceramic, liquid bronze, fine machine engraving and luminous cyan interface glass, ruptured by violet reality corruption; elegant, monumental and tactile rather than generic cyberpunk.”

Palette: Void #05070A; Obsidian #10151C; Ivory #E7E1D5; Bronze #C99A4A; Cyan #56E8F2; Deep Cyan #0C7283; Violet #8C5CFF; Coral #FF5268; White #F4F7F8.

Mara's locked visual target is the supplied concept in production/references/ctrl-reality-concept.png. Preserve a closed ivory mask, elegant slim silhouette, long black segmented coat, fine bronze seams and one cyan glove. The generated angle images are references, not geometry or a claim of perfect multi-view consistency. Check glove laterality, ear-ring detail and panel count before the final asset pass.

Boot Atrium composition: a narrow playable dark deck floats between monumental vaulted bays. Bright ivory faces and fine bronze ribs pull the eye toward distant rings and the Kernel. A local cyan socket is the immediate point of interest. The bridge, not decorative architecture, occupies the navigable center. Floating fragments frame the void. Warm light cuts across engraved dark floor tiles. Haze separates distant architecture.

Camera: guided rear third person; limited mouse orbit, no free camera. Title media is a separate layer and unloads on entry. In-engine Mara remains an articulated development stand-in; cinematic detail is the target, not the current mesh quality. Final assets require the official three-candidate verification and visual-selection workflow.
