# Judge Demo and Pitch

A judge should understand the game before anyone explains its lore.

## Eight-second visual promise

1. Player leaves a pressure plate.
2. Gate closes.
3. Player rewinds.
4. A cyan porcelain copy repeats the route.
5. Copy holds the plate.
6. Gate opens for the current player.

That sequence is the pitch.

## One-sentence spoken pitch

> Every run becomes a programmable teammate, so you rob a clockwork vault by choreographing with your own past attempts.

## Thirty-second live explanation

> You have twenty seconds to perform one role. When you rewind, the room resets, but your completed attempt returns as a Second Hand and repeats exactly what you demonstrated. I use one to hold this plate, another to keep the crank turning, and a third to hit the latch at the right moment, then the current thief crosses the route and takes the jewel.

## Sixty-second hands-on demo route

- 0–10 s: Start and move immediately.
- 10–20 s: Stand on the first plate and rewind.
- 20–35 s: Watch the echo repeat and open the gate.
- 35–50 s: Cross and take the jewel.
- 50–60 s: Show the next chamber with two echo slots and moving hand hazard.

Never begin with repository details or technical claims. Prove the mechanic, then explain that every visible 3D object is editable Three.js code generated through the 404 workflow.

## What should stun rather than merely decorate

- Whole-room mechanical rewind synchronized with the generated parts.
- Three visually distinct Second Hands acting at once.
- Gate, plate, crank, latch, clock hand, and jewel all communicating their logical relationship physically.
- Final shot with current thief and three past selves in one readable composition.

## Demo recovery

At a conference station:

- provide a one-tap reset,
- reset idle stations to title after a reasonable timeout,
- keep a short Chamber 1 path available,
- do not require audio,
- keep controls visible,
- prevent the browser from scrolling or selecting,
- preserve a keyboard route for laptops,
- test weak network by serving a fully self-contained cached/static build.

## Submission copy

Use the prepared three-sentence `what_i_found` in `production/second-hands-entry.json`. Avoid claims such as “first ever,” “AAA,” or “guaranteed winner.” The mechanic and receipts should carry the argument.
