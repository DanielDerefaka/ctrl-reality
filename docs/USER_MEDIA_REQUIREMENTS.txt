ADD THIS AS A NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCTION REQUIREMENT FOR CTRL//REALITY.

The game requires a premium cinematic character introduction, top-quality
character animation, professional music, professional sound effects, and
precise audio mixing.

Do not treat audio and animation as final-hour decoration.

They are part of the game's core presentation and must be implemented,
tested and refined alongside gameplay.

==================================================
1. CINEMATIC STRATEGY
==================================================

Create two related but technically distinct cinematic elements:

A. HIGGSFIELD TITLE CINEMATIC
A six-second seamless image-to-video loop behind the title-screen interface.

B. IN-ENGINE ENTRY TRANSITION
A maximum 1.2-second real-time Three.js transition after the player presses
ENTER THE ARCHIVE.

Do not place a long blocking video between the start button and gameplay.

The player must have movement control no later than 1.5 seconds after pressing
ENTER THE ARCHIVE.

The official game-jam automation presses the start control, waits two seconds,
and then attempts to move the player. Design around that requirement.

==================================================
2. HIGGSFIELD CONNECTION AND SECURITY
==================================================

Use the existing authenticated Higgsfield integration only during development.

Never:
- place Higgsfield credentials in browser code
- put credentials inside game/
- commit credentials
- print credentials in logs
- call Higgsfield from the shipped game
- rely on a temporary Higgsfield output URL at runtime

The Higgsfield integration must:

1. Inspect the current model catalog available to this account.
2. Select a model that supports reference-image video generation and strong
   subject consistency.
3. Use that model's actual current documented request schema.
4. Submit the request asynchronously.
5. store its request_id immediately.
6. Poll the returned status URL.
7. Download completed files into local development storage.
8. Save request metadata without secrets.
9. Record model, prompt, duration, aspect ratio and generation date.
10. Preserve rejected generations as production receipts.

Do not guess model endpoints or parameter names.

6. ENTER THE ARCHIVE TRANSITION
==================================================

When the player presses ENTER THE ARCHIVE:

0.00–0.15 seconds
- resume the Web Audio AudioContext from the real user gesture
- play the UI confirm sound
- lock repeated button activation
- begin fading menu text

0.15–0.45 seconds
- title interface lines contract toward Mara's glove
- cyan interface energy travels into the glove
- title background darkens slightly
- title music begins its crossfade into Atrium ambience

0.45–0.85 seconds
- camera blends from the title framing toward the gameplay camera
- Mara takes one deliberate step forward
- her coat panels settle
- the archive responds with one distant mechanical movement

0.85–1.20 seconds
- the gameplay HUD fades in
- BOOT ATRIUM objective appears
- movement input becomes active
- the camera reaches gameplay framing

Movement must be active by 1.5 seconds at the absolute latest.

The transition must be:
- skippable
- interrupt-safe
- resize-safe
- safe when audio is disabled
- safe when the title video failed
- safe when Reduced Motion is enabled

For Reduced Motion:
- use a 250 to 400 millisecond crossfade
- avoid camera travel
- activate gameplay immediately

10. ANIMATION TIMING TARGETS
==================================================

TITLE_IDLE
4.5 to 6 second seamless loop:
- slow breathing
- tiny shoulder movement
- one subtle head turn
- glove pulse
- almost imperceptible coat motion

ENTER_ARCHIVE
1.0 to 1.2 seconds:
- one controlled step
- head and torso alignment
- camera blend
- no long performance that blocks input

WALK
Approximately 0.9 to 1.05 seconds per full cycle:
- left foot plant
- weight transfer
- right foot plant
- consistent movement speed

CONTROL_PULL
0.55 to 0.75 seconds:
- hand opens toward HUD card
- card detaches
- glove reacts
- body remains balanced

CONTROL_DRAG
Continuous:
- hand aims generally toward the dragged card
- elbow and shoulder remain within natural limits
- glove pulse responds to socket proximity

CONTROL_DEPLOY
0.75 to 0.95 seconds:
- brace stance
- hand pushes card into socket
- socket locks
- hand receives a restrained reaction impulse
- Mara looks toward the resulting world change

REALIGN_FAILURE
1.0 to 1.3 seconds:
- Mara fractures into restrained interface particles
- no gore
- the scene reverses
- immediate checkpoint return

VICTORY
2.5 to 4 seconds after gameplay is complete:
- Mara faces the stabilized Kernel
- the HUD components detach and become part of the Kernel
- Mara lowers her glove
- camera reveals the restored archive
- results interface follows

==================================================

- individual segments produce restrained assembly impacts
- a low architectural resonance grows

- ambience becomes slightly brighter

Do not trigger all layers at exactly the same sample.

19. WIN, LOSS AND CHECKPOINT PRESENTATION
==================================================

FAILURE

Trigger examples:
- Mara falls into the void
- Mara contacts unstable corruption
- the player reaches an invalid timed state

Sequence:
1. 80 to 120 millisecond impact pause
2. coral fracture lines appear
3. Mara and nearby world elements fragment into interface particles
4. failure-fracture sound
5. reverse movement begins
6. failure-reverse sound
7. restore latest checkpoint
8. controls become available immediately

Total duration:
approximately 1.1 to 1.4 seconds.

Do not display GAME OVER.
Do not show a long loading screen.

CHECKPOINT

- thin cyan wave through the floor
- brief glove response
- restrained checkpoint sound
- no full-screen interruption
- update reset location

WIN

After the Kernel is restored:
1. Disable normal movement.
2. Complete the final world transformation.
3. Detach the HUD cards.
4. Install them into the Kernel.
5. Remove the gameplay HUD.
6. Reveal the restored archive.
7. Play the victory animation.
8. Play the completion flourish.
9. Show REALITY STABLE.
10. Open the results interface.

The victory should feel earned and cinematic, not like a generic modal popup.
