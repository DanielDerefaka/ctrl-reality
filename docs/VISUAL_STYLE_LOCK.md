# CTRL//REALITY — production asset style lock

A cinematic impossible archive built from obsidian glass, ivory ceramic, liquid bronze, fine machine engraving and luminous cyan interface glass, ruptured by violet reality corruption; elegant, monumental and tactile rather than generic cyberpunk.

- Obsidian #10151C: tailored black-glass coat and polished structural insets, never pure unlit black.
- Ivory #E7E1D5: closed ceramic mask and architectural curved ribs.
- Bronze #C99A4A: thin warm structural seams, never broad yellow/green paint.
- Cyan #56E8F2: glove, card, socket and active route; restrained emissive detail.
- Void #05070A; White #F4F7F8. Violet is absent from the Atrium asset kit.

Metres. Mara 1.80m high; arch 9m high and 7m wide; socket1.05m high; bridge segment2.8m wide, .50m long, .42m deep including its underside; floor deck13m long by11m wide with believable buttressed underside; ring assembly12m diameter.

Base y=0, centered X/Z, +Z front. Assets export one default function(THREE) returning a Group. No imports, timers, texture files, network, external mesh data or literal vertex blobs. Generated geometry and named articulated pivots only. Use contract material names stone/metal/fabric where appropriate. Surfaces and environment are applied by the runtime.
