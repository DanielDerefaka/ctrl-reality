## Capture rules

- No loading screen, debug UI, editor, cursor, or browser chrome.
- Record at a stable frame rate and crop only after capture.
- Do not fake gameplay with pre-rendered footage.
- Keep the player and echoes large enough for a phone/social crop.
- Trailer is secondary to the working submission; cut it if it threatens the deadline.
