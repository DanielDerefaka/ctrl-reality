## Mara

Three independently constructed garment candidates were rendered in the official five-view verifier. All are 1.8 m tall, grounded and centered. Shared anatomy, mask and hands retain the identity; garment construction differs, not just parameters.

- **01 lofted couture — selected:** continuous fitted torso and curved coat panels give the clearest waist and restrained silhouette; the rear seams and glove rails remain legible. 18,776 triangles in standalone verification.
- **02 tailored profiles — retained/rejected:** planar side profile and angular shoulder shells read too much like folded armor; open skirt reveals an overly mechanical leg arrangement.
- **03 segmented panels — retained/rejected:** layered cuirass and six tails produce busier contours and a wider armored silhouette than the reference.

Named spine, pelvis, head, shoulder, arm, hand, finger, leg, foot and coat pivots survive runtime loading. Distance-driven two-link leg IK replaces time-only walking; coat phase lags the stride and action poses blend over 190 ms. Failure pose and particles reverse, with no moving entry animation while standing still. This is an improvement, not a claim that the concept's sculptural detail has been matched. Mara still reads as a stylized procedural interpretation; user visual approval is required.

Evidence: `candidates/mara/_verify/sheet.png`, individual five-view images, `report.json`, actual desktop/mobile gameplay captures and the direct motion recording.

## Compact platform

- **01 chamfered corbel — selected:** layered bevels, inset circular dial and two believable corbels establish a readable edge and support the crossing.
- **02 separated flags — retained/rejected:** contrasting tiles overpower Mara and read as a checkerboard.
- **03 fan vault — retained/rejected:** awkward intersections and exposed support tips make the silhouette look accidental.

All three passed the official verifier. Selected code is `game/assets/production/recovery-platform.js`; its existing construction is instanced at different depths to form arrival, intermediate landing and the final shallow step. The rest of the socket, bridge, Kernel, mechanism and card families retain their prior three-candidate receipts in `production/asset-receipts/` and are reverified together for this pass.

## Rendering layers and provenance

The declared archive matte remains a distant noninteractive image. Interactive foreground geometry is code-built. Procedural floor grain/inlays, contact-shadow sprite, light halos, glyph projection textures, bridge light surfaces and dust are original local rendering effects, not imported meshes. Continuous motion comes from Kernel rings, dust, Mara idle, channel energy and slow lighting variation. Reflector mirror rendering and disconnected floating arch scraps were removed.

The foreground is still visibly simpler than the matte. It should not be called a locked or approved visual match merely because the structural checks pass.
