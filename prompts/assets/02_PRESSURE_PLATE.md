# Asset 02 — Resonance Pressure Plate

## Purpose

Immediate cause-and-effect object that both player and echoes can hold. It must visibly depress and show power flowing toward its linked mechanism.

## Dimensions

- 1.35 m wide × 1.35 m deep × 0.22 m high.
- Origin at floor center.
- Active travel: top plate moves downward 0.09 m.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated art-deco clockwork pressure plate for a miniature pocket-watch vault, square with clipped corners, aged satin brass frame, inset near-black lacquer base, ivory porcelain central stepping tile, four exposed broad coil springs and small radial gear windows, warm amber light channel around the top, three-quarter view, complete object, no text, no symbols, clean neutral background, even studio lighting.

## Named parts

`root`, `base`, `topPlate`, `springFL`, `springFR`, `springBL`, `springBR`, `indicatorRing`, `gearWindowLeft`, `gearWindowRight`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: square clipped art-deco frame with four visible springs.
- B: circular watch-complication form with radial segments.
- C: minimal porcelain pad suspended in brass rails.

## Animation acceptance

- Top plate depresses without intersecting base.
- Springs compress visually.
- Indicator changes emissive intensity.
- Two small gears counter-rotate.
- Active silhouette remains obvious under player/echo feet.

- Under 6k triangles.
