# Asset 04 — Chronometric Crank Station

## Purpose

A held interaction that teaches timing. Player or echo faces the station and holds ACT; a bridge/ring moves while the crank remains engaged.

## Dimensions

- 0.8 m wide × 0.65 m deep × 1.15 m high.
- Handle radius: about 0.32 m.
- Origin centered on floor footprint.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated waist-high clockwork crank station for an art-deco pocket-watch vault, near-black lacquer pedestal, aged satin brass flywheel and axle, ivory porcelain handle grip, exposed large readable gear pair, jewel-like amber engagement lens, broad bevels, theatrical miniature construction, three-quarter front view, complete object, no labels or electronics, neutral studio background.

## Named parts

`root`, `pedestal`, `flywheel`, `handleArm`, `handleGrip`, `driveGear`, `drivenGear`, `lockingPawl`, `indicatorLens`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: side-mounted flywheel and visible reduction gear.
- B: front ship-wheel silhouette with compact gearing.
- C: vertical winding key with large mechanical cam.

## Animation acceptance

- Handle and gears rotate continuously during hold.
- Pawl visibly engages/disengages.
- Player hands can align near grip from game camera.
- Interaction state remains legible when occupied by translucent echo.

- Under 8k triangles.
- Under 8 draw calls.
