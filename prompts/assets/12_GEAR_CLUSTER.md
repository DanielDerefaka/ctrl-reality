# Asset 12 — Decorative/Functional Gear Cluster

## Purpose

Add controlled mechanical motion and connect mechanisms visually. This is P1 and must be omitted if it threatens schedule or performance.

## Dimensions

- Cluster footprint around 1.4 m × 0.45 m × 1.4 m.
- Large gear diameter 0.8–1.0 m.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated restrained clockwork gear cluster for an art-deco pocket-watch vault, three large readable aged-brass gears with broad teeth, one dark lacquer support frame, ivory porcelain bearing caps, warm recessed mechanism light, no random pile of tiny cogs, clean intentional composition, complete object, three-quarter view, neutral background.

## Named parts

`root`, `frame`, `gearLarge`, `gearMedium`, `gearSmall`, `bearingCaps`, `lightChannel`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: triangular three-gear train.
- B: vertical gear stack with visible drive relationship.
- C: single hero gear plus two restrained supporting gears.

## Animation acceptance

- Teeth appear plausibly engaged.
- Rotation ratios/directions are consistent.
- Can be driven from mechanism state or rewind phase.
- Never blocks player route or becomes visual noise.

## Budget target

- Under 7k triangles.
- Prefer shared materials and clone reuse.
