# Asset 01 — Porcelain Thief Automaton

## Purpose

Hero player and the geometry cloned for all Second Hands. Must read clearly at small phone size and support simple procedural walk/act animation.

## Dimensions and orientation

- Height: 1.05 m.
- Shoulder width: 0.38 m.
- Forward: local negative Z, unless organizer contract requires otherwise; document final convention.
- Origin: floor between feet.
- Silhouette: oversized faceted mask/head, narrow torso, visible brass shoulder/hip joints, short coat-tail shape, compact limbs, one keyhole chest accent.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated full-body miniature porcelain clockwork thief automaton, three-quarter front view, ivory matte porcelain mask and shell plates, lacquered near-black understructure, aged satin brass joints and winding key, slim art-deco proportions, broad readable bevels, expressive but non-human slit eyes glowing faint warm amber, compact feet designed for a top-down mobile game, no weapon, no cape, no text, complete uncropped silhouette, neutral matte studio background, even lighting.

## Named parts

`root`, `body`, `chestShell`, `headPivot`, `mask`, `leftArmPivot`, `rightArmPivot`, `leftForearm`, `rightForearm`, `leftLegPivot`, `rightLegPivot`, `leftFoot`, `rightFoot`, `windingKey`, `chestLens`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: layered porcelain plates over a narrow mechanical skeleton.
- B: single iconic porcelain mask with more exposed brass linkage.
- C: simplified theatrical marionette silhouette with the best phone readability.

## Animation acceptance

- Walk can be made from limb rotations and subtle body bob.
- ACT can extend both arms toward a mechanism.
- Rewind can briefly separate shell parts by 2–4 cm without revealing gaps from the normal camera.
- Winding key can rotate.
- Echo material override can tint shell edges/inner light without destroying part material identity.

## Budget target

- Under 12k triangles preferred, 18k maximum.
- Under 8 draw calls before echo material treatment.
- No finger geometry or facial micro-detail.
