# Asset 08 — Curved Vault Arch and Wall Kit

## Purpose

Frame the board and create an authored room using a small modular set. The wall must not obscure the fixed camera or make the play area too dark.

## Dimensions

- Standard curved bay spans roughly 30 degrees of the 6.4 m radius perimeter.
- Wall height: 2.4 m.
- Wall thickness: 0.32 m.
- Arch opening variant matches vault door dimensions.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated modular curved wall bay for a miniature theatrical pocket-watch vault, lacquered near-black structural panels, broad aged-brass ribs, occasional ivory porcelain inset, restrained art-deco stepped arch, exposed large mechanical fasteners and one warm recessed light slot, clean broad forms, no windows, no text, three-quarter interior view, complete uncropped module, neutral background.

## Named parts

`root`, `wallBody`, `leftRib`, `rightRib`, `topCornice`, `porcelainInset`, `lightSlot`, optional `archFrame`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: panelled curved wall with strong vertical ribs.
- B: stepped theatre-proscenium arch module.
- C: simplest low-draw wall with material insets and premium silhouette.

## Animation acceptance

- Mostly static.
- Optional service panel or light shutter may move, but not required.
- Modules tile without obvious gaps from the game camera.
- Rear/hidden faces may be omitted only when organizer contract and camera guarantee make it safe.

## Budget target

- Under 7k triangles per unique module.
- Use instancing/cloning for repeated bays where compatible.
