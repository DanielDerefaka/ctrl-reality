# Asset 07 — Chronoglass Jewel and Mechanical Pedestal

## Purpose

Final objective and payoff. Must be the brightest controlled accent in the room and support a memorable opening/pickup animation.

## Dimensions

- Pedestal height: 1.05 m.
- Base footprint: 0.9 m diameter.
- Jewel: 0.28–0.34 m tall.

## Reference-image prompt

Isolated theatrical clockwork jewel pedestal, lacquered near-black circular base, aged brass ribs and hinges, six ivory porcelain petal panels opening around a floating faceted pale-cyan Chronoglass crystal, restrained internal warm and cool light, art-deco miniature vault design, strong readable silhouette, three-quarter view, complete object, neutral studio background, no text or logo.

## Named parts

`root`, `base`, `lowerRing`, `upperRing`, `petal01` through `petal06`, `jewelPivot`, `jewel`, `lightCore`, `lockingClaws`.

## Candidate strategies

- A: six-petal mechanical flower.
- B: concentric gyroscope cradle.
- C: minimal three-claw museum pedestal with stronger jewel silhouette.

## Animation acceptance

- Petals/claws open cleanly.
- Jewel rotates and bobs subtly.
- Pickup detaches/hides jewel without breaking pedestal state.
- Use restrained transparency; avoid expensive layered glass artifacts.
