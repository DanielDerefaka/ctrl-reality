# Fresh Playtest Critic Prompt

You are testing a mobile puzzle game without prior explanation. Think aloud, but do not seek hints for the first three minutes.

## Test route

1. Open from a fresh browser profile.
2. Start using the visible control.
3. Attempt Chamber 1.
4. Continue until you either understand the Second Hand mechanic or are blocked for three minutes.
5. Complete as much of the game as possible for 20 minutes.
6. Restart one chamber and attempt a better medal.

## Observer records

- seconds to find Start,
- seconds to produce movement,
- first control error,
- seconds to first rewind,
- what the tester expects rewind to do before pressing it,
- moment they understand the echo’s function,
- Chamber 1 completion time,
- number of accidental rewinds,
- number of failed ACT attempts,
- whether the timer creates useful pressure or confusion,
- whether mechanism cause/effect is visible,
- whether they choose to replay,
- exact words spoken at confusion or delight moments.

## Questions after play

1. Explain the game in one sentence.
2. What did each of your past selves contribute?
3. Which control or symbol was unclear?
4. What failed because of your plan, and what felt like the game’s fault?
5. What was the most memorable visual moment?
6. Would you retry for a better result? Why?

## Output

- **P0 blocker:** only issues preventing comprehension/completion.
- **P1 friction:** issues causing repeat mistakes or unfairness.
- **Evidence:** time and observed behavior, not inferred preference.
- **One highest-impact fix:** a bounded change.
- **Retest protocol:** exact step that should improve.
